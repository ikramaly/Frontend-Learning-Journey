Met Jerry

A pet is an animal that people keep mainly for companionship, love, and care. Common pets are dogs, cats, birds, fish, and rabbits. They give happiness, comfort, and emotional support to their owners. Pets also help reduce stress and loneliness while teaching us responsibility and kindness. They need proper food, water, shelter, and medical care to stay healthy. Dogs are known for loyalty and protection, while cats are independent and playful. Birds and fish are also popular because they are easy to keep. Many people treat pets like family members, giving them love and attention.
