Dr. A.P.J Abdul Kalam

1931-2015

Missile Man Of India

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of the most respected leaders of India. He was born on 15th October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. His full name was Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam. He came from a poor family, but he was very hardworking and intelligent. From childhood, he was interested in science and technology. He studied aeronautical engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology. Later, he joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). He played a major role in developing India’s first satellite launch vehicle. Dr. Kalam is known as the “Missile Man of India” for his contribution to missile technology. He was also involved in India’s nuclear tests at Pokhran in 1998, which made the country stronger. In 2002, Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam became the 11th President of India. He was known as the “People’s President” because of his simplicity and love for students. Even after completing his presidency, he continued to inspire young minds through teaching and writing. He wrote many famous books like Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020. Dr. Kalam believed that youth are the future of the nation. He always motivated students to dream big and work hard. His life was a true example of dedication, honesty, and patriotism. He passed away on 27th July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong. India will always remember him as a great scientist, teacher, and leader.

Biographies

  • Full Name: Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam.
  • Born on 15th October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • Came from a simple and poor family background.
  • Completed aerospace engineering from Madras Institute of Technology.
  • Started career in DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation).
  • Later worked in ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation).
  • Played a key role in India’s first satellite launch vehicle (SLV-III).
  • Known as the “Missile Man of India” for his work on missiles like Agni and Prithvi.
  • Contributed to Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998.
  • Became the 11th President of India (2002–2007).
  • Popularly called the “People’s President”.
  • Great teacher and motivator, especially for students and youth.
  • Wrote famous books like Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020.
